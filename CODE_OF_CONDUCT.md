# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want DockGate's issues, pull requests and discussions to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, **Ali Zeynalli**, may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@Ali7Zeynalli](https://github.com/Ali7Zeynalli)). Reports are kept confidential.

## Scope

This applies in all of DockGate's spaces on GitHub, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
